✅ 1. App Name
Mythic Smashcourt

✅ 2. Subtitle
Push statues, smash enemies, rule the court!

✅ 3. Description
Welcome to Mythic Smashcourt – a fast-paced arcade strategy game where statues become the key to victory!

Fire magical balls to push statues across the arena, smash through obstacles, and outscore your opponent. Each statue that crosses the enemy’s line brings you points – but beware, the enemy is fighting back just as hard!

⚔️ FEATURE HIGHLIGHTS
• Unique statue mechanic – Statues are the scoring pieces. Push them with your shots to claim victory.
• Three skills to upgrade – Power, Speed, Time. Customize your playstyle with strategic upgrades.
• Challenging levels – Progress through 40 increasingly tough levels with smarter enemies.
• Random obstacles – Tombstones spawn unpredictably to block your path, forcing quick strategies.
• Explosive ball duels – Clash balls with the enemy; stronger Power always wins.
• Reward system – Win matches to earn coins, level up, and unlock stronger upgrades.

If you enjoy competitive, skill-based arcade games with a mix of physics and strategy, Mythic Smashcourt is the ultimate battlefield!
